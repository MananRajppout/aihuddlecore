What is AI-Huddle-Core®?

AI-Huddle-Core® is a proprietary, safety-first AI that runs quietly beneath LoveHuddle®. It supports identity integrity, detects risk signals such as fake profiles and romance scams, learns from approved safety intelligence, and calls in human review when judgement is needed. It protects the human connection without becoming the matchmaker.

FAQ

Online dating safety: your questions answered

What is AI-Huddle-Core?

A proprietary, safety-first AI beneath LoveHuddle® that supports identity integrity, detects risk signals, learns from approved safety intelligence and calls in human review.

What is PFN?

PFN stands for People First Network, the human-first framework and engine around AI-Huddle-Core®.

How does AI keep online dating safe?

Through a Safety Gate and Safety Room at entry, then continuous behavioural protection with human oversight.

Does AI-Huddle-Core force matches?

No. It supports safety without becoming the matchmaker, and never scores people.

Can it power other apps?

Yes. LoveHuddle® is the first platform; it is designed as the central engine for future people-first applications.

Is it designed to keep me online longer?
